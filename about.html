Data Science

A meetup for people working at the intersection of data and education. Mostly online, occasionally in London. Roughly once a month.

About this meetup

Built around the intersection.

Why we set it up

There are plenty of events on data science, and plenty on education. Not many bring the two together and tackle the challenges that sit specifically at that intersection — that's what this meetup is for.

What we talk about

Anything where data and education meet — statistics, machine learning, edtech products, data collection, analytics. Methods, research, products and things that didn't quite go to plan.

Who attends

Researchers, data scientists, engineers, educators and product people. Anyone interested in how data informs teaching and learning.
