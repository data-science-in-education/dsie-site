About

A meetup for people who teach
machines that teach people.

We started in 2023 because edtech data scientists kept finding each other at the wrong conferences. This is the right one: small, focused, in the room together.

Our mission

Bridge the gap between data science and education — bring researchers, engineers and educators into one room, and trade what works.

What we talk about

Knowledge tracing. LLM evaluation. Item response theory. Causal inference. Misconception mining. The methods you wish someone had told you about three years ago.

Who shows up

Data scientists from Eedi, Pearson, Sparx and others. PhD students. Curious teachers. Solo researchers shipping odd ideas. Bring questions, leave with collaborators.

Brief history

How we got here.

2023
First meetup

Twelve people in a room above a pub in Old Street. Two talks. Free pizza.

2024
Online + hybrid

Talks streamed to 200+. Recordings start landing on YouTube.

2025
Eedi & friends

Eedi data team adopts the meetup; UCL Knowledge Lab joins as regular venue.

2026
1,000+ members

Members from 14 countries. Blog launched. This year's plan: more practitioner-led talks.
